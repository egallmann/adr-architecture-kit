# Vision ADRs (`ADR-V-*`)

## Status

Vision ADRs are experimental in the public repository surface.

They remain useful for future-state exploration and internal design direction, but they are not part of the stable public v1 contract for `adr-architecture-kit`.

## Purpose

`ADR-V-*` artifacts capture future-state or exploratory architectural direction.

Use them for:

- target-state ideas
- architectural exploration
- staged future capabilities
- design direction that is not yet part of the stable canonical public model

## Relationship to Stable ADR Types

- `ADR-L-*` is the stable logical ADR form
- `ADR-PS-*` is the stable high-level physical-system form
- `ADR-PC-*` is the stable implementation-ready physical-component form
- `ADR-P-*` remains legacy/compatibility
- `ADR-V-*` remains experimental

## Public Guidance

- do not present `ADR-V-*` as co-equal with the stable public ADR taxonomy
- do not build new external dependencies around `ADR-V-*`
- use them as exploratory or historical material unless and until STE promotes them into a stable public contract
