# ADR-V-0001: Conversational Architecture System

## Context

Traditional architecture documentation is:
- Manual and tedious (humans write YAML/diagrams)
- Quickly outdated (docs drift from reality)
- Inaccessible to AI (unstructured, scattered)
- Missing rationale (the "why" is lost)

This creates friction:
- Developers avoid documenting (too much work)
- Architecture becomes tribal knowledge
- AI cannot reason over architecture
- Decisions are lost to history








## Vision Status

This vision ADR is intentionally lightweight and does not yet formalize architectural decisions as `DEC-*` entries.
