# ADR-V-0010: Composable Architecture

**Status:** proposed  
**Created:** 2024-03-10  
**Authors:** adr-architecture-kit  
**Domains:**   
**Tags:** composability, technology-migration, polyglot, modernization  




---

## Context

Technology landscapes evolve:
- New languages (Rust, Go, Zig)
- New frameworks (Next.js, SvelteKit, Astro)
- New databases (Clickhouse, DuckDB, SurrealDB)
- New paradigms (serverless, edge computing)

Traditional approach: Big bang rewrites
- Rewrite entire system in new technology
- High risk (everything changes at once)
- Long timeline (months to years)
- Expensive (full team dedicated)

Problems:
- Risky (all or nothing)
- Slow (can't ship features during rewrite)
- Expensive (opportunity cost)
- Often fails (abandoned mid-rewrite)

Opportunity: Incremental migration through composability.








## Vision Status

This vision ADR is intentionally lightweight and does not yet formalize architectural decisions as `DEC-*` entries.
